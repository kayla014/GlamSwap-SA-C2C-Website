# GlamSwap-SA---C2C-E-commerce-Website
GlamSwap SA is a C2C fashion marketplace for buying, selling, and renting formal outfits across South Africa. GlamSwap SA connects people who want to sell or rent out their formal wear with people looking for outfits for an upcoming event. Built as a full-stack PHP/MySQL web application, with a custom gold/black/cream brand identity.
